The short version. The marketing site sets no cookies and runs no analytics or trackers. The application stores your account, your dashboards, and encrypted API credentials for the services you choose to connect. Self-hosting keeps all of it on your own infrastructure, with no third party — including us — in the path.

1. Controller

Malango Tech UG (haftungsbeschränkt), registered address, Germany.
Email: privacy@usebussola.com. Full company details are in the imprint.

If a data protection officer has been appointed, name and contact them here.

2. What this covers

This policy applies to two different things, and the distinction matters:

  • The website at usebussola.com — these marketing pages.
  • The hosted application at usebussola.app — accounts, dashboards and connections.

It does not apply to self-hosted installations. If you run Bussola on your own infrastructure, you are the controller for everything it stores, and no data reaches us.

3. The website

These pages set no cookies, embed no analytics, and load no third-party scripts or fonts. One preference — your light or dark theme choice — is stored in your browser's local storage and never leaves your device.

Our hosting provider processes standard server log data (IP address, requested page, timestamp, referrer, user agent) to deliver the site and defend it against abuse. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in operating a secure website, Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR. State the log retention period your host applies.

4. Account data

When you create an account on the hosted application we process:

  • Your email address and a hashed password
  • Your organization and membership records
  • Session records, so you stay signed in

The legal basis is performance of a contract, Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR. This data is kept while your account exists and deleted when you close it, subject to the retention obligations in section 9.

5. Connected services and their data

When you connect a source, you provide an API credential for that service. It is encrypted with AES-256-GCM before storage, used only to make read requests on your behalf, and never returned to the browser or exposed through the MCP server. See the security page for the full handling.

What we store from those services is deliberately narrow:

  • The latest snapshot per connection — statuses, counts and aggregate figures
  • One history sample per connection per hour, pruned to your plan's retention

Snapshots can contain personal data if your own systems return it — a Stripe payment description, a bank transaction counterparty, the author of a deploy. In that case you are the controller of that data and we process it on your instructions as a processor, under Art. 28 GDPR. Link the data processing agreement here once it exists.

Deleting a connection deletes its credential and its snapshots.

6. Billing

Paid plans are processed by Stripe. Card details are entered on Stripe's own checkout and never reach our servers; we store the Stripe customer and subscription identifiers, the active plan, and the invoices Stripe issues. Legal basis: performance of a contract, Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR, and our legal obligation to keep accounting records, Art. 6 (1) (c).

The services you connect — Railway, Stripe, Qonto and the rest — are not our processors. You hold those relationships directly; Bussola only reads from them with the credential you supply.

8. Retention

  • Account data: while the account exists, then deleted
  • Credentials: until you delete the connection or the account
  • Snapshots and history: pruned continuously to your plan's window — 7 days on Trial, 30 days on Solo, 12 months on Team
  • Invoices and accounting records: statutory period, typically 10 years under German law

9. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you (Art. 15)
  • Have inaccurate data corrected (Art. 16)
  • Have your data erased (Art. 17)
  • Restrict processing (Art. 18)
  • Receive your data in a portable format (Art. 20)
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21)
  • Withdraw consent at any time, without affecting prior processing

Write to privacy@usebussola.com and we will answer within one month. You also have the right to complain to a supervisory authority — for us, the competent Landesdatenschutzbehörde.

10. Changes to this policy

We will update this page when the product changes in a way that affects it, and note the date below. Material changes to the hosted service will also be announced by email.
