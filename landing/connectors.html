How connections behave

Careful with your tokens, and with theirs

Encrypted at rest

Every secret is sealed with AES-256-GCM before it touches storage, and never written to a log in the clear.

Tested on add

Bussola makes a live call when you connect a source, so a bad or under-scoped key fails now rather than at render time.

Polite when it breaks

A failing connection backs off exponentially to at most hourly and stops after ten failures, so a revoked token cannot get you rate-limited.

Missing the one you need?

The connector layer is open source and deliberately small: a fetch function, a shape, a widget. Open an issue, or send the pull request.
