Connectors

Connect everything you already use

Every connector stores its credentials per user, AES-256-GCM encrypted, and is tested with a real request before it is saved.

API-key based. No OAuth app, no review queue, no waiting.

Encrypted at rest

Every secret is sealed with AES-256-GCM before it touches storage. Nothing is logged in the clear.

Tested on add

Bussola makes a live call when you connect a source, so a bad key fails now — not silently at render time.

Cached politely

Widget responses are cached ~45–60s per tenant to keep upstream rate limits and local load low.
